Questions

What is a GSE macro?

GSE (GnomeSequencer Enhanced) plays a sequence of spells on one key, one spell per press. Ronin gives you ready sequences for your class: press one key and the rotation follows the priority.

Does it work in Midnight?

Yes. The Retail pack is made for Midnight (12.x) and checked against the current class guides.

WoW: Forever too?

Yes. Tank, DPS and Heal from level 1 to 60, in the same add-on.
