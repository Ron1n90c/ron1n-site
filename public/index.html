What you get

Retail (Midnight)

  • TANK every tank spec, both hero trees
  • DPS Death Knight, Demon Hunter, Druid, Hunter, Mage, Monk, Paladin, Shaman, Warlock, Warrior
  • HEAL Mistweaver, Holy Paladin, Resto Druid, Resto Shaman

Versions: Mythic+, Raid, Delve, Beginner, Leveling.

WoW: Forever

  • TANK Paladin, Warrior, Druid
  • DPS Paladin, Warrior, Druid, Hunter, Shaman, Warlock, Mage, Priest
  • HEAL Paladin, Druid, Shaman, Priest

Level ranges 1-10, 10-40 and 40-60.

Easy installer

Type /ronin, pick your world and role, tick your classes, import. Every class shows Up to date, Update available or Not installed.

Heal: hover a frame and hold the key. It heals the friend under your mouse, otherwise you.

How to start

  1. Install GSE, then Ronin
    CurseForge app, or download the zip above.
  2. Type /ronin
    Pick Retail or Forever, then Tank, DPS or Heal, and import.
  3. Bind it in GSE
    Put the macro on your bar and play.
