Tested: every macro

Before every release each Ronin sequence is run in a simulation, every version on its own. The number is how much of the global cooldown the macro keeps busy when you press the key 8 times per second and 4 times per second. 90% or more is our bar. Version 4.4.0-beta1 (Retail pack 7.3.3, Forever pack 4.1.2), tested 27 November 2026.
