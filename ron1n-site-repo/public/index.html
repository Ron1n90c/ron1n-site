How it works

Small steps, real priority.

GSE’s Priority mode picks a step on every press, and earlier steps are picked more often. With six steps, step 1 gets 6/21 of the presses and step 6 gets 1/21. The important spells sit first, the rare ones last.

Blood DK, one step (castsequence style)

/castsequence reset=4 Blood Boil, Death Strike -- resets after 4 s, so it can never get stuck

Every step stays under GSE’s 255 character limit after compiling. A talent you don’t have is skipped.

How it works

Sequential, short, no ranks.

Forever macros run as sequential steps: each press fires the next one and the list loops. Spells are written by name, never by rank or spell ID, so learning a new rank doesn’t break the macro. Spells you haven’t learned yet are skipped.

1→2→3→4→5→6↺

Forever’s macro engine has no buff or debuff conditions, so refreshes use timed castsequences instead.

Protection Paladin, step 1

/targetenemy [noexists][dead] /cast [mod:shift,@player]Flash of Light;[mod:alt]Hammer of Justice;[mod:ctrl]Rebuke /castsequence reset=combat/2 Seal of Fury, Judgement, Judgement, Judgement /startattack
Rules of the house

Built to tank, not to play for you.

In the macro

  • Your threat and damage rotation, with active mitigation where it belongs
  • Heal and CC on modifier keys
  • Smart refreshes with castsequence timers

Left out on purpose

  • Big defensives. You press those, because you know when you need them.
  • Trinkets and any DPS rotation.
  • Anything that needs rage, health or stagger readouts. Macros can’t read them.

Install

Three steps, one command.

  1. Copy the addon folder
    Put RoninInstaller into your AddOns folder and make sure GSE is enabled.
  2. Import
    Log in, type the command, tick the classes you want and click Import Selected.
  3. Put the macro on your bar
    Drag the GSE macro to your action bar and bind it. After every GSE update, place it on the bar again.

    FAQ

    Quick answers.

    Nothing casts after a GSE update.

    A GSE update unbinds the macro from the action bar. Drag it onto the bar again and bind it. The sequences themselves are not affected.

    Does it play the game for me?

    No. It presses the next sensible spell on every key press. You still position, taunt, use cooldowns and call the pull.

    My game isn’t in English.

    Spell names are translated and can become longer. Check the character counter in the GSE editor: every step has to stay under 255 characters.

    Why are the big defensives missing?

    Because timing them is the tank’s job. A macro that burns Shield Wall on cooldown is worse than no macro.

    A spell is in the wrong order.

    Tell me which class and level you play and what feels off. The sequences are small on purpose, so changes are quick.
